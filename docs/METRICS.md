# MVP Success Metrics

## North Star Metric
**Qualified Leads Generated:** Number of teams with >$500/mo in potential savings who submit their email.

## Secondary Metrics
1. **Audit Completion Rate:** % of users who start the form vs. view the results page. (Target: >80%).
2. **Total Identified Savings:** Cumulative dollar amount of wasted spend found by the tool. (Great for PR/Marketing).
3. **Share Rate:** % of results URLs that are visited by a 2nd unique IP address (indicating the champion shared it with their team/finance).

## Phase 1 Goal
- 500 audits completed.
- 50 qualified leads captured.
- 5 closed deals for Credex discounted credits.
