# User Interviews & Findings

## Hypothesis
Teams are buying multiple AI tools without fully understanding the overlapping features, leading to wasted spend.

## Interview 1: CTO, Series A Startup (25 Eng)
- **Current Stack:** GitHub Copilot for all engineers, ChatGPT Plus for all engineers, Claude Pro for 5 seniors.
- **Pain Point:** "I don't know who is actually using what. We just approve the expenses because 'AI makes them faster'."
- **Reaction to Audit Concept:** "If you can tell me I can drop ChatGPT for the engineers because Claude Team does the same thing better, I'll do it today."

## Interview 2: VP Engineering, Mid-market (100 Eng)
- **Current Stack:** Cursor for some, Copilot for others. No centralized policy.
- **Pain Point:** "We have 3 different IDE setups. The billing is a mess."
- **Reaction to Audit Concept:** "I'd love a shareable report I can just hand to Finance to justify moving everyone to one standard tool."

## Key Takeaways
- The value is in *clarity* and *confidence*.
- The report needs to look official enough to be shared with Finance.
- Emphasize the "redundancy" aspect (e.g., paying for both Copilot and Cursor).
