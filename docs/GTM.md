# Go-To-Market Strategy: Credex AI Audit Tool

## The Problem
Companies are rapidly adopting AI tools (ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot, Claude) but lack visibility into redundant spending. Engineering managers and finance teams are overpaying for overlapping subscriptions.

## The Solution
A free, instant "AI Tool Audit" that identifies wasted spend and recommends optimal setups.

## Target Audience
1. **Engineering Managers / CTOs:** Care about developer productivity and budget optimization.
2. **Finance Managers / RevOps:** Care about cutting unnecessary SaaS spend.

## Acquisition Channels
1. **Product Hunt Launch:** Positioned as "Find out how much you're overpaying for AI."
2. **LinkedIn Content:** Share anonymized examples of crazy AI tool overlaps (e.g., "Why 80% of teams paying for Cursor and Copilot are burning cash").
3. **Cold Email (Founders/CTOs):** "Can we save you $5,000/yr on AI tools? Take this 2-minute audit."

## The Credex Upsell
The audit is the top-of-funnel magnet. Once we show a company they are wasting $X/mo, the call-to-action is to capture those savings by purchasing discounted AI credits through Credex.

## Funnel
1. Traffic -> Audit Form
2. Audit Results -> "You're overpaying by $X"
3. CTA -> "Talk to Credex to get 40% off your optimal AI stack."
4. Lead Capture -> Sales Conversation -> Close.
