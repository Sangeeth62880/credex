# Unit Economics — Credex AI Audit

## Lead Value
A converted lead is worth an estimated **$1,500** to Credex.
**Reasoning**:
*   An "audit lead" is pre-qualified. They have a high AI spend and are actively looking for efficiency.
*   Credex sells AI credits at scale. A typical Series B team might spend $50k/year on API credits.
*   Credex takes a 3% margin/fee on credit procurement = $1,500 LTV per customer.

## CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)
*   **Organic Social (X/LinkedIn)**: $0 (Time-intensive).
*   **Community Inbound**: $0 (Trust-based).
*   **Widget Embeds**: $0 (Network-effect).
*   **Estimated Blended CAC**: **$10–$25** (primarily compute costs and human outbound time).

## Conversion Funnel
To be profitable, we need the following conversion rates:
1.  **Audit Completed → Lead Captured**: 15% (Providing a "Download PDF" or "Get Credits" incentive).
2.  **Lead Captured → Consultation Booked**: 10% (Automated email sequence via Resend).
3.  **Consultation Booked → Credit Purchase**: 20% (High-touch sales closer).
*   **Total Audit-to-Customer conversion**: 0.3%.
*   If we get 1,000 audits, we get 3 customers = **$4,500 Revenue**.
*   Cost to run 1,000 audits (Groq + Vercel + Resend) ≈ $50.
*   **Net Profit per 1k Audits: $4,450.**

## Path to $1M ARR in 18 Months
To hit $1M ARR ($83k/mo), we need:
1.  **55 new customers per month** (at $1,500 LTV).
2.  This requires **18,500 audits per month** (at our 0.3% conversion rate).
3.  **How to achieve 18k audits/mo?**
    *   **Embed Strategy**: Get the "Credex Audit Widget" onto the top 50 "AI Tool Directory" sites.
    *   **SEO**: Rank #1 for "AI Tool Comparison" and "Best AI for coding" through the `PRICING_DATA.md` content.
    *   **Partnerships**: Integrate with VC portfolio "perks" pages (Y Combinator, Sequoia, etc.).
