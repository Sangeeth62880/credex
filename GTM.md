# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy — Credex AI Audit

## The Target User
The exact target user is the **Head of Engineering** or **VP of Finance** at a **Series A or Series B SaaS startup** (20–100 employees). 

At this stage, the company has enough employees that "AI subscriptions" have started to fragment across departments. The founder is no longer approving every $20/mo expense, and the "AI Tax" is likely reaching $2k–$5k/month in waste. This user is incentivized to find "quick wins" to extend runway without laying off headcount.

## Search & Behavior Triggers
Right before they want this tool, they are:
1. **Googling**: "Cursor vs GitHub Copilot Business features," "Claude Team vs Pro billing," or "How to cancel ChatGPT Team subscription."
2. **Scrolling**: Through "SaaS Cost Optimization" threads on X or reading "2026 AI Tool Guide" in the *Lenny’s Newsletter* Slack community.
3. **Checking**: Their Mercury or Brex dashboard and noticing 15 different charges from "OpenAI" and "Anthropic."

## Distribution Channels
*   **Subreddits**: `r/startups`, `r/CFO`, and `r/SaaS`. We won't just "post"; we will answer questions about tool comparisons and offer a link to the "redundancy checker."
*   **Slack/Discord**: *Operators Guild*, *FinOps Foundation*, and *Venture Scale*.
*   **X (Twitter)**: Founders like @levelsio or @tobi who frequently discuss lean operations.

## The First 100 Users ($0 Budget)
We will execute a "Manual Outbound Value" play in 30 days:
1.  **Days 1-10**: Identify 500 Series A startups. Find their Head of Eng on LinkedIn.
2.  **Days 11-20**: Send a personalized DM: *"Saw your team is hiring for AI engineers. We built a 2-min tool to audit AI spend. Average Series A team is overpaying by $3k/yr. Here's a link if it helps: audit.credex.rocks"*
3.  **Days 21-30**: Partner with **"SaaS Stack" influencers** on X. Offer them the "Benchmark Data" (e.g., "The average startup spends $45/dev on AI") for their next post in exchange for a shoutout.

## The Unfair Distribution Channel
**Credex's Existing Network**. Credex is already a platform for financial credits. We will embed the AI Audit as a "Free Health Check" directly inside the Credex dashboard. Every existing user becomes a lead for the audit, and every audit becomes a lead for Credex’s core credit-purchase business.

## Week 1 Traction Goals
*   **500 Total Visits** (via organic X posts and LinkedIn DMs).
*   **100 Audits Completed** (20% conversion).
*   **15 Lead Captures** (15% lead conversion from optimized reports).
*   **3 High-Value Consultations Booked** for Credex.
