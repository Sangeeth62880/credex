**Q: How accurate is the pricing data?**
**A**: We manually verify our pricing database every week against the official vendor pages (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.). You can see our sources and verification dates in the `PRICING_DATA.md` file in our repository.

**Q: What tools do you support?**
**A**: We currently support the "Big 8": Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, Anthropic API, OpenAI API, Gemini, and Windsurf. We are adding more monthly based on user requests.

**Q: Is the report really free?**
**A**: Yes. The audit is 100% free. Credex makes money when you choose to use our platform to purchase enterprise AI credits at scale, but the audit report is yours to keep regardless.

**Q: Can I share the report with my CFO?**
**A**: Absolutely. Every audit generates a unique, shareable URL and a print-optimized PDF version designed specifically for financial stakeholders.
